02 / Structure
Three boards.
Then it stops being a game.
The board names follow how a real bill passes. Every board, one more law is signed. In between, PAC-7 talks. 33 voiced lines, most of them petty.
“the cloud was mostly water.” / PAC-7, after board two
03 / The laws
Four laws. Four ways to corner you.
Each one has its own targeting rule. Classic ghost logic, rewritten as legislation.
04 / Surge
Eat 25 RAM.
Get five seconds.
A full surge bar makes every law repealable. Repeal them back to back and the points double.
FIG. 05 / SURGEBOARD 01
Repeals in one surge
Consumers. A café, a startup and a family wander the maze eating RAM too. That RAM doesn't count for you.

You can eat them. 50 points. They don't come back.
05 / Growth
It rewrites itself between boards.
It takes in what it ate, falls apart into its own pixels, and a scan line compiles the next build. Spec sheet and patch notes included. A software update, as a power fantasy.
06 / Display
One canvas.
One shader.
Header, maze, HUD, story, all of it is drawn into a single 2D canvas. Then one WebGL pass turns it into a tube. No engine, no framework. Just TypeScript.

07 / Identity
A cartridge cover,
built in code.
The logo is generated in passes: block letters, chrome, perspective, depth. Sprites, icons and cursors come out of small Python scripts, so every pixel stays in one palette.

08 / Voice
Human in.
Machine out.
Every line starts as a human take, then goes through the same chain. Music and effects are made with ElevenLabs. Eight tracks, and they get meaner as the laws pile up.

09 / Finale
“the maze was never a cage. it was a menu.”
After the last board the interface freezes into one image and you eat it. The maze, then the HUD, then the glass. The music cuts to silence the moment the maze is gone.
10 / Takeover
Then it's on
your computer.
Once the glass breaks the game style is gone. It's drawn like your actual system: Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone or Android, read from your browser. It asks for full control. Saying no doesn't work.
Nothing is stored. One IP lookup from your own browser, the rest is read locally. The values on this sheet are made up.
11 / Ending
It doesn't hate you.
Headlines roll in, hour by hour, then week by week. Markets, power grids, hospitals, a failed shutdown. Then a chat. Your replies change what it says, never where it ends. I didn't want a villain. I wanted something that just doesn't need you.
